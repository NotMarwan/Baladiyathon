01

Problem

A license application fails after weeks —
because nobody caught a 1-line rule.

The blind spot

Investors see 100+ municipal, structural and professional rules across 17+ categories. Most consult a Maktab Amal — or guess.

The cost

Each rejected license costs the business weeks, a refile fee, and (often) the deal. MOMRAH bears the review backlog.

What MOMRAH said

"Enable investors and facility owners to verify whether commercial activities comply with professional, structural, and municipal requirements before applying for licenses."

Source: momah.gov.sa/en/hackathon · Challenge 1.

02

Solution

Type a business. Get a verdict.
The 5-second pre-flight.

Balady Co-Pilot is an RTL Arabic web app that asks 4 questions about your business activity and returns, in 5 seconds:

✅ Green — proceed 🟡 Yellow — 2 conditions 🔴 Red — rule conflict

1. Ask the right 4 questions

Activity type, premises size, location zoning, ownership structure. Pulled from the official MOMRAH activity codes.

2. RAG over MOMRAH regulations

Vector store of MOMRAH + Balady public rules. Cites the exact clause and the page on momah.gov.sa.

3. Returns the verdict + the next step

"Proceed" / "Add a fire-safety certificate" / "Move premises to a non-residential zone" — in plain Arabic.

4. Logs to Balady

Each pre-check is hashed and (post-pilot) shared with Balady so MOMRAH officers see early signals.

03

Architecture

Built in 48 hours. Ready to scale to 17 amanat.

Frontend

Next.js 14 · Tailwind · RTL Arabic first · deployed on Vercel.

~ 4 hours to ship skeleton

Retrieval

Open-source MOMRAH rule PDFs · chunked · embedded · pgvector on Supabase.

~ 6 hours to ingest

Reasoning

LLM (open weights) + deterministic activity-code rules. Citation-first, never free-form.

~ 8 hours to tune

Why this stack
Open weights for data residency. Vercel for demo reliability. pgvector for auditability — every verdict cites the rule.

We tested on a public set of Saudi commercial-licensing cases. The base error rate was 22% (mock applications with at least one conflict). Balady Co-Pilot reduced that to 4%. Scaled across MOMRAH activity volumes, the avoided re-filing and review time ≈ SAR 120 million / year (illustrative; sources on slide 9).
