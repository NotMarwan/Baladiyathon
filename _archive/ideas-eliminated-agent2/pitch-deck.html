The Solution

Raqeeb AI predicts which establishments are most likely to violate municipal codes, then explains why — so inspectors act before citizens complain.

  • Risk Score per Establishment — prioritizes inspections by probability of violation
  • SHAP Explainability — every flag shows the evidence, building inspector trust
  • Geospatial Heatmap — district-level view for resource deployment
  • Feedback Loop — inspection results continuously retrain the model

How It Works

1. Ingest Balady data, complaints, weather, and seasonal signals.

2. Train a LightGBM/XGBoost classifier on historical inspection outcomes.

3. Generate daily pre-inspection lists with explainable risk drivers.

4. Inspectors confirm or refute; model learns and improves.
