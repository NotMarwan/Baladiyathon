# Agent 4 — Safety, Quality & Scams

## DANGEROUS INTERACTIONS

- **St. John's Wort + CYP3A4 drugs** · Evidence: Strong · Induces CYP3A4/CYP2C19/P-gp — slashes levels of birth control, warfarin, cyclosporine, statins, SSRIs, HIV drugs · Organ rejection reported in transplant patients · Source: https://examine.com/supplements/st-johns-wort/
- **SSRIs + St. John's Wort / 5-HTP / L-Tryptophan** · Evidence: Strong · Serotonin syndrome risk (hyperthermia, rigidity, myoclonus) · Source: https://examine.com/supplements/st-johns-wort/
- **Blood thinners + Vitamin E (>400 IU), Ginkgo, Garlic, Fish Oil (>3g EPA/DHA)** · Evidence: Moderate · Additive bleeding; warfarin INR unpredictable with St. John's Wort · Source: https://examine.com/supplements/st-johns-wort/
- **Levothyroxine + Ashwagandha** · Evidence: Moderate · May increase T3/T4; thyrotoxicosis case reports; destabilizes hypothyroid dosing · Source: https://examine.com/supplements/ashwagandha/
- **Oral contraceptives + St. John's Wort** · Evidence: Strong · CYP3A4 induction → reduced ethinyl estradiol → contraceptive failure · Source: https://examine.com/supplements/st-johns-wort/
- **Caffeine + CYP1A2 inhibitors (fluvoxamine, ciprofloxacin)** · Evidence: Strong · Clearance drops 2-5x; toxicity risk · Limit: ≤400mg/day adults; ≤200mg pregnancy · Source: https://examine.com/supplements/caffeine/

## QUALITY CERTIFICATIONS — What Actually Matters

- **USP Verified** · Identity, potency, disintegration, heavy metals · ~200 products carry it · Source: https://www.usp.org/
- **NSF Certified for Sport** · WADA banned-substance testing + GMP audit · Gold standard for athletes · Source: https://nsf.org/certification/dietary-supplements
- **Informed Sport** · WADA-compliant batch testing; lot-numbers verified online · Source: https://www.informed-choice.org/ [UNVERIFIED]
- No third-party cert on a pre-workout or T-booster? Assume undeclared stimulants or prohormones. Caffeine supplements found adulterated with banned substances. · Source: https://examine.com/supplements/caffeine/

## RED FLAGS ON LABELS

- **"Proprietary Blend"** · Hides doses; fairy-dusting legal · Never buy
- **"Clinically Proven" without citation** · If they can't name the study, it doesn't exist
- **"Standardized Extract" without marker compound %** · Standardized to what?
- **Study dose buried in a blend** · 600mg ashwagandha study dose ≠ 600mg in a 2g blend
- **No third-party certification** · Adulteration documented, especially pre-workouts/hormone products
- **"Natural" as a selling point** · Cyanide is natural

## TL;DR

**Worth taking**: Third-party certification (NSF/USP/Informed Sport) before ANY purchase; pharmacist check if on SSRIs, blood thinners, thyroid meds, or birth control.

**Skip**: Proprietary blends without individual doses; testosterone boosters; detox products; uncertified pre-workouts.

**What most people get wrong**: "Natural = safe." St. John's Wort is natural and will destroy your birth control, destabilize warfarin, and potentially reject your transplanted organ. Check interactions at https://examine.com before stacking anything with meds.
